01 — Microsoft Entra Login Screen

The screen users see when they click the invitation link. Entra ID allows: header banner image, body text, button colour (limited — Microsoft blue is default; peach CTA shown below as ideal), and footer links.

For Henry: The "Next" button colour in Entra ID is controlled by the "accent colour" setting in the branding config — set to #EF7B6C (LotCheck peach). The header banner should use the white-on-dark logo variation on the dark grey background (#2B3D48). The card itself is always white — Microsoft constraint.

03 — Builder Invitation Email (via Estate)

Sent when an estate approves a builder. Names the specific estate. Works harder to explain the value and set expectations — the ask (upload plans) requires real effort.

Outlook / Gmail
You've been invited to list your plans on Hamilton Rise
From: LotCheck <noreply@mail.lotcheck.com.au>
To: James Harper <james@designhomes.com.au>
lotcheck.

Hi James,

Hamilton Rise has invited you to list your floor plans on LotCheck.

LotCheck is a planning tool that helps buyers visualise how house designs fit on specific lots. When a buyer is browsing Hamilton Rise, your matched plans appear alongside the lot — generating qualified enquiries directly to you.

To get your plans in front of buyers, you'll need to upload them to your builder profile. It takes about 20–30 minutes for an initial set of plans, and you can add more at any time.

Set up your account →

Once you're in, you'll be able to:

  • Review Hamilton Rise's design guidelines
  • Upload your floor plans (name, dimensions, storeys, key features)
  • See which of your plans match available lots

If you have questions before getting started, just reply to this email.

The LotCheck Team
